Keyboard shortcuts and notes

Keyboard shortcuts

These work anywhere on the page except in the text box and the speed slider.

  • Space play or pause
  • ← and → back or forward one word
  • Shift + ← back to the start of the sentence (press again to go back another sentence)
  • Home restart from the beginning

How timing works

The speed sets the base time for each word. Words ending a clause (comma, semicolon, colon, dash) get extra time, sentence endings get double time, and paragraph breaks pause a little longer. Long words also get a bit more time. Because of these pauses, your actual reading speed will be a little below the number shown.

Before you use it

At the top speed, the word changes around 12 times a second. If fast changing text is uncomfortable, start slow and pause at any time.
